## Overview

A project exploring big data analytics and financial econometrics, including risk–return analysis and quantitative investing across equities, futures, options, and cryptocurrencies. Conducted during April–May 2026.

## My work

- Studied asset pricing, modern portfolio construction, and risk–return analysis.
- Applied descriptive statistics, portfolio analysis, and Fama–MacBeth regressions to study common equity factors and strategy performance.
- Explored big data and dimensionality reduction techniques in cryptocurrency and commodity futures strategies.
- Completed an approximately 1,500-word research report and a final presentation.

## Methodological perspective

The project connects question formulation, data analysis, and performance evaluation. Factor research requires separating statistical relationships from executable strategy performance; portfolio results also need to be interpreted in relation to risk, sample periods, and trading costs.
